## Part 1: Participant information sheet

### 1. Study title

Content validation of the AI Data Centre Facilities Assurance Framework (AIDCF): expert panel using a modified Delphi method. Protocol AIDCF-VAL-002 v1.0.

### 2. Invitation

You are invited to take part because of your professional experience in `[discipline]`. Please read this information before deciding. You may ask the facilitator any question before or during the study.

### 3. Purpose

AIDCF is an open draft framework for assuring AI data-centre infrastructure. This study asks a panel of 8 to 12 practitioners whether a sample of its controls and all of its Critical Gates are relevant, clear, testable and correctly framed. The results will be used to revise the framework and will be published in anonymised form. The study tests the framework; it does not test you.

### 4. What participation involves

- Two or three rounds of an online or file-based questionnaire, over approximately `[number]` weeks.
- Round 1: rating 48 controls and 15 gates on 4-point scales with optional comments; about 3 to 4 hours, which you may spread over 14 days.
- Rounds 2 and 3: re-rating only items on which the panel has not agreed, after seeing anonymised group results and reasons; about 1 to 2 hours each.
- No meetings, interviews or recordings.

### 5. Do I have to take part?

No. Participation is voluntary and unpaid `[or: describe any honorarium]`. Not taking part, or withdrawing, has no consequence for you.

### 6. Anonymity

- The facilitator, `[name, organisation]`, will know your identity. Other panellists will not, and the framework author will not see your identified responses.
- Your responses will carry only a code (for example P07).
- Other panellists will see only group statistics and anonymised summaries of reasons. Your comments will be checked and edited to remove anything that could identify you before they are shared or published.
- Published results will describe the panel only in aggregate (for example the number of panellists per discipline). Discipline-level results will be published only where at least three panellists share a discipline.
- Because the professional community is small, complete anonymity cannot be guaranteed if you tell others you took part.

### 7. Confidential information

Please do not provide confidential information about any identifiable site, organisation, customer, vendor or incident, including your employer's. Give your professional judgement in general terms. Any such content received will be deleted and you will be told.

If your employer's policy requires permission for you to take part in external technical review, you are responsible for obtaining it.

### 8. Data held about you

| Data | Purpose | Held by | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name, contact details | Contacting you during the study | Facilitator | Until the final report is published, then destroyed, unless you agree below to be contacted for future review |
| Discipline, summary of experience, independence status | Confirming eligibility; aggregate reporting | Facilitator | As above |
| Conflict declaration, this consent form | Records of the study | Sponsor | `[period set by sponsor on data-protection advice]` |
| Coded ratings and comments | Analysis and audit of published results | Sponsor | `[period set by sponsor on data-protection advice]` |

Data controller: `[sponsor legal name and contact]`. Data-protection contact: `[contact]`. Lawful basis and your data-protection rights: `[to be completed by the sponsor's data-protection adviser]`.

### 9. Withdrawing

You may withdraw at any time without giving a reason, by telling the facilitator. You may choose whether ratings already given are kept or deleted. Deletion is possible until the anonymised results of the round in which you gave them have been circulated to the panel. After that, your ratings are combined in group statistics and cannot be removed.

### 10. Risks and benefits

The main burden is time. The main risk is accidental disclosure of identity or of confidential information; the safeguards above address this. There is no direct benefit to you. You may receive the published results, and your aggregated contribution may improve the framework.

### 11. Results

The anonymised analysis report will be published in the AIDCF repository under `validation/expert-panel/`. You will be sent a copy. Results will not be presented as endorsement, accreditation or certification of AIDCF, and you will not be named as having endorsed it.
