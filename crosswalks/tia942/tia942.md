## 1. Scope of ANSI/TIA-942

ANSI/TIA-942 specifies minimum requirements for the telecommunications infrastructure of data centres: spaces (entrance room, main distribution area, intermediate, horizontal and zone distribution areas, equipment distribution area), cabling topology, pathways, redundancy and administration, with reference to companion TIA standards (for example the TIA-568 cabling series, TIA-606 administration and TIA-607 bonding and grounding). It also provides guidance on site selection, architectural and structural, electrical and mechanical infrastructure, and defines four rated levels (Rated-1 to Rated-4) expressing increasing redundancy and resilience across those subsystems. Third-party conformity assessment schemes for TIA-942 exist; the standard itself is a design standard.

## 2. Where it overlaps with AIDCF

- **NET-05 Physical connectivity and optics** is the strongest overlap (cabling, cross-connects, pathways, administration).
- **Facility subsystems.** Electrical (POW-01 to POW-04), mechanical (THM-01, THM-02, THM-04), architectural (PHY-01 to PHY-04) and redundancy of paths (DEP-02, DEP-03, RES-02) are PARTIAL at design level.
- **Rated levels** provide a design-resilience intent that is CONTEXT for CAP-02, CAP-03 and RES-01.

## 3. Where AIDCF goes beyond ANSI/TIA-942

- TIA-942 is a design and construction standard. It does not address operation, maintenance, change, incident or capacity management (OPS), continuity and recovery testing (RES-03 to RES-06), supply chain (SUP), digital security (SEC) or evidence and decisions (EVA).
- It addresses physical cabling, not the logical design, performance and fault domains of AI scale-up and scale-out fabrics (NET-01 to NET-04, NET-06).
- It does not address accelerator compute, AI storage, liquid cooling at chip level or AI load transients at a confirmed level.
- A rated level is not a Service Outcome. AIDCF requires tolerances, operating-state coverage and claim-type evidence.

## 4. Using ANSI/TIA-942 outputs as AIDCF evidence

| Output | AIDCF evidence class | Useful for | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Third-party TIA-942 conformity certificate or audit report (rated level per subsystem) | E5 | PHY, POW, THM, NET-05 DESIGN and IMPLEMENTATION claims | Design and as-built at a point in time; per-subsystem ratings may differ; no operational coverage |
| Cabling design, labelling and test records (with TIA-568/606 practices) | E1/E4 | NET-05 Cabling Standards, Path Documentation, Cross-Connect Control | Physical layer only; optics compatibility for AI fabrics is AIDCF-specific |
| Entrance pathway and access-provider diversity drawings | E1 | DEP-03 Telecom Dependency; DEP-02 Shared Route Analysis | Physical diversity must be verified to the provider network, not only to the property line |

## 5. Gaps and cautions

- The overall rating of a facility under TIA-942 is limited by its lowest-rated subsystem. AIDCF assessments should use per-subsystem ratings, not a headline rating, when mapping to POW, THM and PHY evidence.
- Section and annex numbers are not cited because they differ between revisions; confirm against the revision in use.
