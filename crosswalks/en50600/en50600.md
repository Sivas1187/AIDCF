## 1. Scope of EN 50600

EN 50600 is a multi-part European standard for the planning, construction and operation of data centre facilities and infrastructures. Parts used in this crosswalk:

| Part | Subject | AIDCF domains most affected |
|---|---|---|
| EN 50600-1 | General concepts: design principles, business risk analysis, availability classes, protection classes, energy-efficiency enablement | CAP, RES |
| EN 50600-2-1 | Building construction | PHY |
| EN 50600-2-2 | Power distribution (supply and distribution) | POW |
| EN 50600-2-3 | Environmental control | THM, ENV-01 |
| EN 50600-2-4 | Telecommunications cabling infrastructure | NET-05 |
| EN 50600-2-5 | Security systems (protection against unauthorised access, fire and other events) | PHY |
| EN 50600-3-1 | Management and operational information | OPS |
| EN 50600-4-x | Key performance indicators (overview, PUE, renewable energy factor, cooling efficiency ratio, carbon usage effectiveness, water usage effectiveness and others) | ENV-02 to ENV-04 |

The ISO/IEC 22237 series is the international counterpart and covers the same subjects with its own part numbering; users relying on ISO/IEC 22237 should confirm the corresponding part numbers against the edition in use. Certification against EN 50600 is offered by third-party schemes; the standard itself does not define a certification scheme.

## 2. Where it overlaps with AIDCF

- **Facility domains.** EN 50600-2-x overlaps PHY, POW and THM families at design level, structured by availability class (1 to 4) and protection class. POW-01, THM-01, PHY-04 and NET-05 are among the HIGH-confidence PARTIAL mappings.
- **Operations.** EN 50600-3-1 defines operational and management processes (including incident, change, capacity and maintenance processes) and maps to all six OPS families.
- **Energy and environmental KPIs.** The 4-x series provides standardised definitions of PUE, REF, CER, CUE and WUE that AIDCF ENV-02 to ENV-04 can adopt as measurement methods.
- **Criticality.** The business risk analysis in EN 50600-1, which selects availability and protection classes, overlaps CAP-03.

## 3. Where AIDCF goes beyond EN 50600

- **Class versus outcome.** An availability class describes design redundancy of infrastructure. AIDCF asserts that a Capability Instance achieves Service Outcomes within tolerance across operating states (including DEGRADED, MAINTENANCE and RECOVERY) and requires OPERATION or EFFECTIVENESS claims with evidence, not only DESIGN.
- **AI-specific thermal and electrical behaviour.** Transient ride-through and load-step response of synchronous accelerator workloads (POW-05), direct-to-chip liquid cooling, CDUs and secondary loops (THM-03), and coolant quality (THM-05) are not addressed, or not addressed at a confirmed level, in the parts mapped.
- **AI compute, storage and network.** EN 50600 stops at the facility and cabling infrastructure; CMP, STO and NET (except physical cabling) are outside scope.
- **Dependency and concentration.** Common controllers, shared human dependencies, supplier and knowledge concentration (DEP-02, DEP-04) are AIDCF-specific.
- **Security, supply chain and evidence.** Digital and OT security (SEC), supply chain (SUP) and evidence management (EVA) are not EN 50600 subjects.

## 4. Using EN 50600 outputs as AIDCF evidence

| Output | AIDCF evidence class | Useful for | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Third-party EN 50600 conformity certificate and audit report | E5 | PHY, POW, THM design claims at declared availability class | Point-in-time; design and construction scope; does not cover AI load behaviour or current configuration |
| Business risk analysis and class selection record | E1/E7 | CAP-03, RES-01 | Must be linked to Service Outcomes and tolerances |
| Design documents per part (2-1 to 2-5) | E1 | PHY, POW, THM, NET-05 DESIGN claims | Reconcile to as-built (CG-02) |
| 3-1 operational records (incident, change, maintenance) | E3 | OPS families OPERATION claims | Coverage of operating states and periods |
| KPI measurements per 4-x | E3/E7 | ENV-02 to ENV-04 | Measurement boundary and category must match the AIDCF energy-allocation boundary |

## 5. Gaps and cautions

- Several parts have been revised since first publication, and some subject matter has moved between parts (for example management and operational information). Confirm the edition of each part in use.
- IT-equipment KPI parts in the 4-x series are cited at series level in CMP-03 because the author did not confirm part numbers; confirm before use.
- Life safety, egress and emergency shutdown are governed by national fire and building regulation; EN 50600-2-5 provisions are PARTIAL for PHY-06 only.
