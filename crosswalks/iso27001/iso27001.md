## 1. Scope of ISO/IEC 27001

ISO/IEC 27001 specifies requirements for an information security management system (ISMS): context and scope, leadership, risk assessment and treatment, support, operation, performance evaluation and improvement (clauses 4–10). Annex A lists 93 reference controls in four themes: organisational (5.1–5.37), people (6.1–6.8), physical (7.1–7.14) and technological (8.1–8.34). Controls are selected through risk treatment and recorded in a statement of applicability (SoA). Implementation guidance is in ISO/IEC 27002:2022. Certification attests that the ISMS conforms to the requirements for the stated scope.

## 2. Where it overlaps with AIDCF

ISO/IEC 27001 has the broadest overlap of any crosswalked framework because its Annex A spans governance, supplier, physical, operations and technical security controls.

- **Governance and accountability (GOV).** Clauses 5, 6.1, 9.2, 9.3 and A.5.1–A.5.6, A.5.31, A.5.35–A.5.37. Risk-owner acceptance of residual risk (6.1.3) maps to GOV-02 Risk Acceptance Authority.
- **Digital, OT and control-plane security (SEC-01, SEC-03 to SEC-05).** Access control, identity, privileged access, network segregation, vulnerability management, logging and monitoring, incident management (A.5.15–A.5.18, A.5.24–A.5.28, A.8.2, A.8.5, A.8.8, A.8.9, A.8.15, A.8.16, A.8.20–A.8.23) are HIGH-confidence PARTIAL mappings.
- **Physical security (PHY-03 to PHY-05).** A.7.1–A.7.4, A.7.9, A.7.10, A.7.14.
- **Operations (OPS-02 to OPS-05).** Documented procedures (A.5.37), maintenance (A.7.13), incident management, configuration and change management (A.8.9, A.8.32).
- **Suppliers (DEP-03, DEP-06, SUP-01 to SUP-04, SUP-06).** A.5.19–A.5.23.
- **Continuity (RES-01, RES-03 to RES-05).** A.5.29 and A.5.30 (ICT readiness for business continuity), A.8.13 and A.8.14.
- **Evidence and assessment (EVA).** Documented information (7.5), collection of evidence (A.5.28), internal audit (9.2), nonconformity (10.2).

## 3. Where AIDCF goes beyond ISO/IEC 27001

- **Supporting utilities are a single control.** A.7.11 covers protection from power and other utility failures at objective level. AIDCF devotes two full domains (POW, THM; 48 controls) to the engineering of electrical supply and heat removal across operating states, with Critical Gates CG-05 and CG-06 at sufficiency floor S5.
- **Control planes beyond IT.** AIDCF SEC-02 addresses BMS, EPMS, cluster manager, fabric controller and storage controller security as named control planes; 27001 applies generic controls. Use the [ISA/IEC 62443 crosswalk](../isa62443/isa62443.md) for OT depth.
- **AI-enabled action.** SEC-06 (agent or automation authority) has no 27001 counterpart beyond privileged access and change control.
- **AI infrastructure performance and integrity.** CMP, STO and NET families concerned with performance, scale-up domains, collective communication and checkpoint durability are outside an ISMS.
- **Capability and outcome.** 27001 protects information assets' confidentiality, integrity and availability; AIDCF asserts capability outcomes within tolerance under states, with UNKNOWN Records and gate results.

## 4. Using ISO/IEC 27001 outputs as AIDCF evidence

| Output | AIDCF evidence class | Useful for | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| ISMS certificate and certification-body audit report | E5 | GOV, SEC, OPS-04, OPS-05 (existence and operation of processes in scope) | Scope and SoA exclusions; sample-based; period-bound |
| Statement of applicability | E1 | Identify which Annex A controls are in scope; spot exclusions affecting SEC and PHY families | Selection, not effectiveness |
| Risk assessment and treatment plan | E1/E7 | GOV-04, CAP-03 | Information-security risk; not capability outcome risk |
| Asset inventory (A.5.9) | E1/E2 | CAP-01, DEP-01 seed | Reconcile to as-built (CG-02) |
| Logs, access reviews, vulnerability scans, incident records | E2/E3 | SEC-01, SEC-04, SEC-05, OPS-04 | Must cover OT and AI-infrastructure control planes in the AIDCF boundary |
| ICT continuity tests (A.5.30) | E4 | RES-04, RES-05 | Usually IT-system restoration only; not integrated power/cooling/compute scenarios |

Check specifically whether the ISMS scope includes facility OT (BMS, EPMS, CDU controllers) and AI infrastructure management planes. If it does not, ISMS evidence is not relevant to SEC-02 or CG-07, CG-15.

## 5. Gaps and cautions

- Annex A control numbers are those of the 2022 edition; mappings using the 2013 edition numbering (A.5–A.18) are not valid here.
- PARTIAL relationships in the SEC domain are HIGH confidence for IT scope only; OT scope requires explicit confirmation.
